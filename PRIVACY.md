# Privacy Policy: Tab Brainrot Meter

The privacy policy lives at **https://tab-brainrot.vercel.app/privacy**. Use that URL in the Chrome Web Store listing and in Dodo Payments.

Its source is [`site/privacy.html`](site/privacy.html). Edit it there, so there's only one version to keep accurate.

**In short:** your tabs never leave your browser. The extension sends one thing over the internet, your Pro license key, and only to Dodo Payments to check it. The extension has no analytics, ads or trackers. The website sets no cookies and counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't identify visitors.
