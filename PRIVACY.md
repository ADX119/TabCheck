# Privacy Policy: Tab Brainrot Meter

_Last updated: October 2026_

Tab Brainrot Meter does not collect, sell or share your browsing data.

## What stays on your device

- **Tab count.** Read locally to show your brainrot level. Never sent anywhere.
- **Tab graveyard.** When you close tabs with the extension, their titles and URLs are saved in your browser's local extension storage so you can restore them. You can clear it at any time, and it is deleted when you uninstall the extension.
- **Notification state.** Which tab count you were last notified about, kept for the current browser session only.

## Payments

Pro upgrades are handled by [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com) and [Stripe](https://stripe.com). When you check the paid status or buy Pro, the extension talks to ExtensionPay using a random user id. Payment details and your email go directly to ExtensionPay/Stripe and are never seen or stored by this extension. See the [ExtensionPay privacy policy](https://extensionpay.com/privacy).

## Donations

The donate link opens an external site. Anything you do there is covered by that site's privacy policy.
