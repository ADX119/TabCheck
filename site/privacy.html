Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version: your tabs never leave your browser. The extension sends one thing over the internet, your Pro license key, and only to Dodo Payments to check it. We don't use analytics, ads, cookies or trackers, in the extension or on this website, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Tab Brainrot Meter (the Chrome extension and this website) is made and run by Aditya Andurkar, an individual developer based in India. In this policy “we” and “us” mean Aditya Andurkar. We are responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at tabcheckenquiry@gmail.com about anything in this policy, including requests and complaints.

When the extension talks to Dodo Payments

  • When you enter a key, it sends the key and a label for this browser (for example “Chrome 2026-10-28”) to activate it.
  • About once a week, it sends the key and its activation ID to check it hasn't been refunded or disabled.
  • When you click “Remove key”, it sends the key and activation ID to free up that activation.

Nothing else is sent: no tabs, no browsing history, no device identifiers.

Permissions, and why each is needed

  • Notifications: to tell you when you cross a tab threshold.
  • Storage: to keep the closed-tab list, notification state and license key on your device.
  • Tabs (optional): asked for the first time you run a cleanup, so the extension can see tab titles and addresses. Counting tabs doesn't need it. You can turn it off any time in chrome://extensions.
  • Access to live.dodopayments.com and test.dodopayments.com: to check license keys. No other sites.

The extension never reads the content of the pages you visit, never runs code downloaded from the internet, and contains no analytics or advertising code.

When you buy Pro

Pro is sold by Dodo Payments, which acts as our merchant of record and reseller. When you pay, you give your name, email address, billing country and address, and payment details directly to Dodo. Dodo's privacy policy covers how Dodo uses them.

Dodo shares the following with us so we can run the product: your name, email address, country, what you bought, the amount and date, your order ID and the status of your license key. We never see your card or bank details. We use this information only to deliver your license, answer support requests, handle refunds and disputes, prevent fraud and keep the records the law requires.

This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and loads nothing from other companies: no analytics, fonts, ads or embeds. That's why there's no cookie banner. There's nothing to consent to.

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

When you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you. Our email provider stores these messages on our behalf.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract: activating and checking your license and providing Pro.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the website and licensing secure, preventing fraud and answering your messages.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records for tax and accounting.
  • Consent: the optional tabs permission, which you can withdraw in chrome://extensions.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers above: Dodo Payments (payments and license keys), Vercel (website hosting) and our email provider. We don't sell or rent personal data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles. We may disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and India. Where the law requires it, they use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you clear it in the extension or uninstall the extension, which deletes everything.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 8 years.
  • Support emails: until your request is resolved, then for up to 24 months.
  • Website request logs: held by Vercel for a short period under its own policy.

Your choices and rights

You control the data on your device:

  • Clear the closed-tab list from the graveyard in the extension.
  • Click “Remove key” to delete your license key from the browser.
  • Turn off the tabs permission in chrome://extensions.
  • Uninstall the extension to delete all of its data.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export the personal data we hold about you, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Email tabcheckenquiry@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the email address you bought with so we don't hand your data to someone else.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority. In India that's the Data Protection Board of India. In the EEA it's your local supervisory authority, and in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office. California residents: we don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

Children

Tab Brainrot Meter isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. Pro must be bought by an adult, or by a minor with a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child has sent us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Everything the extension runs is bundled into it; it never loads code from the internet. A strict content security policy stops it from contacting any site except Dodo Payments. There are no secret keys in the extension, and every connection uses HTTPS. The source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check these claims. To report a security problem, see the contact page.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Tab Brainrot Meter's use of information it receives through Chrome's extension APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Tab data is used only to provide the extension's features, is never transferred to anyone, and is never used for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to those features.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what data leaves your device, we'll also announce it in the extension's release notes before it takes effect.
