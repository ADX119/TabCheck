Five levels of cooked

Your level updates live as you open and close tabs. Every tab past ten costs you 100 aura.

  1. 0–10 Sigma Mode 🗿 “Sigma grindset. Your RAM is mewing.”
  2. 11–25 Mid Behavior 😐 “It's giving ‘I'll read it later’. You won't.”
  3. 26–50 Lowkey Cooked 🍳 “Bro thinks the tab bar is a bookmark manager.”
  4. 51–100 Skibidi Brainrot 🚽 “Your tabs need their own zip code.”
  5. 101+ Tung Tung Tung Sahur 🪵 “You don't have tabs. You have a lore.”
