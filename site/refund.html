Refund Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

14 days, no questions asked. If Pro isn't for you, email us within 14 days of buying and you'll get your money back in full.

How to get a refund

Email tabcheckenquiry@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase. Send it from the email address you bought with, or include that address, and add your order ID from the Dodo Payments receipt if you have it. You don't need to give a reason.

What happens next

  • We confirm your request within 3 business days.
  • Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, sends the full amount, including any tax charged, back to the payment method you used. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • Your license key is disabled. Pro stops working in the extension at its next license check, within about a week. The free features keep working.

After 14 days

Pro is a one-time digital purchase that starts working immediately, so we don't normally refund it after 14 days. We'll still refund you if Pro doesn't work for you and we can't fix it, or if you were charged twice by mistake. Just email us.

Chargebacks

Please email us before disputing a charge with your bank. It's faster for you, and we'll always refund a valid request. A disputed payment disables the license key.

Cancellations

There's nothing to cancel: Pro is a single payment, not a subscription, and you'll never be charged again.

Your legal rights

This policy is in addition to any refund or withdrawal rights you have under the consumer laws where you live. It doesn't replace them.
